VOID · logo concepts

8 marks in the cell language. Each card shows the mark at 16, 24, 32 and 48 px, a one-colour version on light, and the app icon.

01 · EscapeYour current ring, one cell breaking free. Asymmetry reads as motion; the gap it leaves is the void.
02 · Event HorizonThe ring fades clockwise into a violet core. It looks like it is spinning even when it isn't.
03 · Hollow BlockA Minecraft block with its core carved out. The game and the word "void" in one shape.
04 · PortalA stepped pixel O around a glowing core. It is the O in VOID and a portal at the same time.
05 · Strike VA V monogram in stepped cells that lands on an orange hit cell, like a sword strike.
06 · CritA 3×3 grid with the center deleted and the corners blown out. The hit moment as a glyph; survives 16px.
07 · DissolveA clean ring that breaks into cells. A modern client with a Minecraft soul.
08 · BracketFour corner brackets around one violet cell. A crosshair and a focus target; very calm.
