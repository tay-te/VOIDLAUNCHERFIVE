What the cards lost

Pack cards dropped the mod count — you do not choose a pack by how many mods it has, and it was already on the hero for the selected one. The “Selected” tag went too: the gradient and the white play button already say that. Padding, badge and type all came down a step.

That bought a third row. Nine packs are visible instead of six, in the same space, on smaller cards — which is the point, since a real library is not six packs.

One grid, three meanings

Every page is the same shape: a hero column that acts, a 3×3 grid that lists, a deck that shows background work. Only the content changes — packs, search results, friends.

Learn the screen once and you know all three.

Browse answers the real question

The thing that goes wrong when installing mods is where they land. So the last card in the Browse hero is a permanent Installing into target with a Change button — visible before you click a single plus.

Installed mods say Installed rather than offering a plus that does nothing.

Friends is sorted by what you can do

People you can join right now come first and stay full-strength; offline friends dim to 62% and their action button is disabled with a clock rather than removed — the row keeps its shape.

The big number is 3 online, not 14 friends, because online is the number you act on.

The deck persists

Downloads stay in the bottom-left on all three pages, so the nav’s 12 badge always has somewhere to point. The right half is contextual: Discover, Collections, Shared with you.
