The six mechanics, and the numbers behind them.

The grain never moves

The noise is fixed to the window, so cards slide under a stationary film. Attach it to each card instead and it reads as a sticker — the illusion dies. Highest-leverage decision here.

#grain{position:fixed;inset:0; mix-blend-mode:overlay;opacity:.55}

Key travel

Hover raises the key 3px→5px and brightens it. Press drops it 2px, collapses the contact shadow to zero and inverts the bevel, so it bottoms out against the surface.

:active{transform:translateY(2px); box-shadow:0 0 0 0, inset 0 3px 5px rgba(0,0,0,.42)}

The cursor travels

The accent marker slides between rows instead of teleporting. This single detail does more for the machine feeling than anything else on the screen.

transition:transform .22s var(--detent)

Detent, not bounce

Tabs settle with about 4% overshoot — a switch clicking into a notch, not jelly. Same curve on the rail cursor and the field underline.

--detent:linear(0,.006,.025,.101, .539 .5,.826,.934,.981,1.001,1.004,1)

Grooves close a contact

The field is cut into the surface. Focus deepens the inner shadow and wipes an accent line along the bottom edge from the centre out.

:focus-within::after{transform:scaleX(1)}

Digits tick

Counts count up in tabular mono so nothing reflows while they move. Click a pack row to watch the mod count run to its new value.

font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums
