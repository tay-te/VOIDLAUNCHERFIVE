Browse had to change shape

Browsing is inherently many-at-once, which fights focus. So the search field became the subject — 52px, centre stage — with a quiet keyboard list beneath and a live preview of whatever is highlighted.

The preview answers the questions that decide an install: does it fit this pack, what else does it need, when was it last touched. Iris shows Requires: Sodium; nothing else in the list does.

The install target sits inside the search bar, so where a mod lands is never a guess.

Friends has a real subject

Not a roster — the person you can act on. Online friends put Join on the stage; offline ones swap it for Message, so the page never shows a dead button.

Underneath sit the three facts Join actually raises: who is already in that world, how long you two have played together, and whether you own their pack. All three change per person.

The strip is the whole navigation

One row, one job per page: switch pack, switch content type, switch person. Pills are 32px; every other control is 20–30px, and only the primary action reaches 38.

Where it will hurt

Nine packs fit the strip; forty will not. It needs overflow behaviour — scroll, or a “more” pill that opens the command bar filtered to packs.

And the whole design leans on ⌘K existing and being good. Managing 342 mods cannot live on this stage.
