# VOID Hearth structure audit, October 2026

The question: is every Hearth structure high quality, hand-built, detailed and very playable? Can a player and the
folk get in, move round, use the beds, stairs, doors and workstations, in rooms that are well lit, with nothing
floating, blocked or cramped? This report combines three sources for all 134 registered archetypes:

1. a measured audit of every blueprint (`structureAudit`),
2. six auditors' visual and source review, one per family group,
3. a walk in the real game with the real player and the folk's own pathfinder.

It also folds in the second art review (`ART_REVIEW_2.md`, whole towns, branch `hearth-w7-review` @ `0d63e7ef`). It
ends with a fix plan in file-disjoint lanes, each lane with acceptance checks the audit tool can verify. The audit
gate's thresholds are now in `BUILDING_GUIDE.md` §8.

Evidence is in [`audit/`](audit/):

- `findings/G1-homes.md` … `G6-land.md` and `findings/walkthrough.md`: the auditors' and the walk's full reports, with
  crops (WebP).
- `INDEX.md`, `archetypes.csv`, `worst.md`, `top.txt`: the measured audit's index, per-archetype scores and worst cases.
- `sheets/`: eight of the contact sheets, the most telling ones. The full set is regenerated by
  `structureAudit -Pmode=all`. Links in the findings to other sheets point at that output.
- `walk/`: `walk_results.md` (every building, room, path and defect with coordinates), `walk_summary.tsv`, and nine
  screenshots.

Snapshot: branch `hearth` at `6acb5a67` (wave 6 merged), the audit tool at `7c8a23b3`. Since then, `hearth-w6-fix` (the
castle and keep rework) has been merged into `hearth` (`a6d8d47c`), so the **castle rows below describe the castle
before that fix**. Re-run them before acting on them.

## 1. Verdict

**Not yet at the bar. No archetype grades A.** Hearth builds well-lit, grounded and mostly sound buildings. Nothing
floats. There is one invariant-check failure in 10,356 cases (a pre-fix castle). Zero room cells are under light 8 in
the measured audit, and 94 % of reached interior cells are at light 8 or more in game. The real player walked 1059 of
1059 paths without sticking. The ground floor works almost everywhere. What fails the bar:

- **Things people cannot use.** 24 % of suited cases (2,481 of 10,356) have at least one blocking defect: a furnished
  room nobody reaches, a door with no floor on one side, a broken flight, a ladder with nowhere to step off, a bed or
  workstation with no free face, or a chest that cannot open. 75 of 134 archetypes have at least one such case. Doors
  alone fail in 1,214 cases across 37 archetypes. The most visible are the market hall (no usable way to the guild
  hall, in every culture), the windmill (no mill floor reachable, in every culture) and the courtyard house (wings
  sealed off by pots).
- **The folk cannot climb what the player climbs.** In game, the folk's pathfinder failed 59 times to reach beds and
  work places on upper storeys that the player reaches: merchant house 4/4 cultures, tenement, manor, townhouse,
  farmhouse lofts and keep. At midnight 14 of 48 residents of the grown town were not in bed. Five of them, from five
  different homes, stood at one stuck point.
- **Drops everywhere.** 39 % of cases (117 archetypes) have walked cells beside an unguarded drop of 4 or more: flat
  roof terraces, galleries, palace arcade roofs (140 to 350 cells), pagoda tiers, belfries opening onto roofs, lofts
  and staging.
- **Not yet hand-built.** In 91 archetypes one plan is reskinned across five or more cultures (`culture_likes` >= 5;
  review 2's similarity check flags 85 of 121). Facades are flat: the median largest flat patch is 30 cells against a
  line of 24, and the monastery, university, caravanserai and stables have walls of 150 to 218 cells. The desert and
  mesa small buildings are one crenellated cube. Mesa is desert block for block in all 23 civic types.

Scores (means over suited cases): play 86.9 / 100, detail 70.8 / 100. Only 29 of 134 archetypes reach play >= 90,
worst case >= 75 and detail >= 75.

| archetypes | 0 | 11 | 10 | 30 | 30 | 29 | 7 | 6 | 10 | 1 |

**Blockers: 56 entries** (§4). 8 of them were found only in the game walk. Several entries cover many archetypes

## 2. Method

**Severity.**

- **Blocker:** broken or unplayable. A room, bed, workstation, door, stair or POI that a player or the folk cannot
  use, or a defect a player sees at once (sails through a tower).
- **Major:** clearly not hand-built quality, or a real playability cost: drops, cramped rooms, sealed storeys under a
  furnished facade, one plan in many cultures.
- **Minor:** polish.

**Grades** are per archetype across its cultures:

- **A:** at the bar.
- **B:** good, with small fixes.
- **C:** needs rework.
- **D:** broken in places or far below the bar.
- **F:** does not work.

The auditors' grades stand, with three exceptions where the game walk proved more than the sheets showed:

- market hall C to D (walk FAIL in 4 of 4 cultures),
- merchant house C+ to C (walk FAIL in 4 of 4),
- the mine keeps C but gains a blocker.

**Known limits and metric quirks.**

- **Castle and keep rows predate the `hearth-w6-fix` merge.**
- Infrastructure draws a stub on a bare plot. The dock's demo on `coast` runs over land. Deep buildings sit on a
  synthetic cliff. G6 judged these families from the real planner and the deep previews instead.
- Tropical and karst lattice or shutter openings count as no window.
- Round plans score detail 0 (the round house).
- Decorative cauldrons, such as the well's bucket, count as unusable workstations.
- The tavern's lantern tie beams read as a floor.
- **BlueprintChecks' walker stands on flower pots, candles and lanterns. The light check's walker does not.** So the
  tests pass on buildings that are sealed in play (the courtyard house). This is cause S3 in §5.
- The folk-path check in the walk uses a detached `hearth:folk` entity with follow range 48.

## 5. Systemic causes

Ranked by how many defects each one explains. Fix these once, in the kits, and most of §4 and the majors go with
them.

**S1. Doors are placed without checking both sides.** This is the largest single cause:

- 1,214 cases in 37 archetypes have a door with a wall, a drop, a post, a rail or furniture on one side, and 387
  cases have doors nobody walks to.
- Kit side: `TownHouses.galleryDoor` and `Fronts.plainDoor` check only the cell below. `Partitions.door` and the
  `StairCore` landing doors check nothing. `Porches.balcony` is one block deep with the rail in front of the door. The
  window dressing (flower boxes, shutters) is drawn in the door's column before the door.
- Furnishing side: `Rooms.furnish`, the counters and the ovens put a chest, a bench, a counter or a smoker in front of
  a door (cottage, carpentry, market hall, bakery).
- Explains H2-H3, H8, H10-H14, C1, M1, M3, M4, W3, W8, W13 and the monastery, convent, university and observatory
  door majors.
- Fix: one `Doors` rule. Place a door only where both sides have a standable floor with two air above and nothing in
  the way. Clear that column's window dressing. Otherwise leave a window. Every furnishing pass keeps both door
  fronts.

**S2. Upper storeys are built, but nothing checks that you can get there.**

- `Hall.terrace` builds its stair only on one storey (W1).
- `Gate.java`'s ladder stops short of roofed heads (N1).
- The windmill's spiral passes under floors 4 apart (W2).
- The croft hatch sits at the eaves (H6).
- The carpentry, storehouse and caravan-yard lofts have no ladder.
- The watermill and counting house give up when a flight does not fit.
- Belfries open onto roofs, so the walk climbs roof stairs: the courthouse, chapel and town hall `bad_flights`.
- Flights turn without a landing. The player jumps across; the folk's pathfinder does not (H5).
- 2,144 cases in 58 archetypes have whole storeys no stair reaches. Most are sealed attics under roofs, which cost
  hidden lamps and read as floors from outside.
- Fix:
  - a flight or ladder is placed only if the walk reaches its top;
  - every turn of a flight gets a landing;
  - a loft is either given a ladder with a landing cell or opened to the room below (rafters showing) or sealed.

**S3. Furniture and props are placed without keeping paths and faces, and the test walker is laxer than a player.**

- `workstations_unusable` fails in 637 cases, `chest_lids_blocked` in 177, `beds_no_free_side` in 25.
- Rooms with no 3x3 of clear floor: 5,092 cases in 95 archetypes. Cramped rooms: 1,741 cases.
- Examples: the courtyard court, the alchemist still, the brick kiln furnace, the smelter tap, chests under gallery
  shelves and hut roofs, cellar corner casks, beds placed after the stores.
- BlueprintChecks' walker stands on flower pots, candles and lanterns. So H1 and N5 pass the tests while players and
  folk are shut out.
- Fix:
  - `Rooms.furnish` places beds first with a free long side, keeps station faces and door fronts, never puts a chest
    under a solid block, and leaves a 3x3 in every room of 9+ cells;
  - BlueprintChecks walks as the light check does (`Spaces.standable`).

**S4. Walkable edges have no guard.**

- Fall-edge cells in 4,034 cases (39 %) in 117 archetypes: flat roof decks in desert, mesa, karst and tropical (30-86
  cells), palace arcade roofs (140-350), pagoda tiers (101-128), cathedral and bell-tower galleries (36-105), hayloft
  wells, shipyard staging, tailor and smelter galleries, deep dwelling stairs, pueblo terraces, mine cuttings,
  stairwells.
- `Adobe.parapet` is applied to side ranges but not to the house deck. Belfry openings have no sill.
- Fix: a shared guard (parapet, balustrade or rail of the culture's railing) on every walkable edge over a drop of 3+.
  Gaps only where a ladder, stair or hoist serves it. Intended edges (quarry faces, quays, the town side of a wall
  walk) are listed as exceptions.

**S5. One plan, reskinned, and a thin vocabulary in the dry and tropical cultures.**

- `culture_likes` >= 5 in 913 of 1,726 flat cases, in 91 archetypes. Review 2: 85 of 121 flagged.
- Mesa is desert block for block in all 23 civic and law types (`Tradition` maps both to `ADOBE`).
- The desert and mesa hut, hovel, hermit hut, shack, croft, cliff dwelling and the small civic types are one
  crenellated cube.
- Tropical walls are lemon-yellow bamboo planks. Steppe town homes are four-storey gabled mud-brick blocks (off-culture).
- Misfits: the wisteria "pagoda" is the bell tower, the bayou temple a Chinese hall, the mesa temple a Mesoamerican
  pyramid.
- Fix: per culture group, 2-4 plans per archetype that matters in a town (review 2 §6 ranks which); a `PUEBLO`
  tradition for mesa; an Adobe vocabulary (vigas, canales, rounded parapets, ramadas, stepped volumes); a palette pass
  for tropical.

**S6. Flat facades and exposed party walls.**

- `flat_patch_max` is over 24 cells in 4,743 cases in 94 archetypes.
- Monastery, convent and university are at 191-218 cells; stables 145-208; caravanserai 160-180; warehouse 151;
  merchant house and townhouse side walls 99-125.
- Relief is under 0.06 in 1,975 cases: playhouse 0.00, masonry 0.02, shophouse 0.01-0.07, university 0.04.
- Town homes designed for a terrace stand detached, so their party walls face the street (review 2 items 3 and 9 agree).
- Fix: plinth, string courses, frames, sills, posts or buttresses every 3, and regular window bays. A detached town
  home dresses its flanks.

**S7. Interiors too small, too low or too empty.**

- The hovel, hermit hut, shack and hut have 1-3 free floor cells.
- The deep dwelling sleeps 6 beds wall to wall. The lighthouse cottage is 3x3.
- Tropical, karst and maritime storeys are 3 high, so heads touch the ceiling (`tight_headroom_share` over 0.35 in 218
  cases; 1.0 in the tropical stilt house, school and tavern).
- Empty rooms: university chambers, town hall and courthouse ground floors, bathhouse, toll gate, watchtower storeys,
  karst and bayou temple halls, the warehouse ground floor.
- Fix: grow the small bodies or drop the second bed; 4-high storeys in those habits; furnish by room purpose.

**S8. Tests and previews do not see the real case.**

- No test asks the folk's pathfinder (H5 and D2 were found only in game).
- The dock demo runs over land. The walkway is a stub. Deep buildings preview on a synthetic cliff.
- Non-walkable roof holes are not counted (the wisteria rectory).
- Floating pieces are not checked (review 2: walkway decks, fair-ground trees and tilt-yard fences on slopes).
- Fix: lane L0.

The acceptance thresholds every architect runs before committing are in `BUILDING_GUIDE.md` §8, "The audit gate".

**The gate (lane L0, wave 7).** What this report found the tests passing is now checked in them (`build/check/PlayChecks.java`,
inside `BlueprintChecks.all`): the invariant walk with the light check's footing (`walk`), a standable cell on both sides
of every door (`door-sides`), a step-off at every ladder top (`ladder-top`), every furnished room reached
(`furnished-reached`), no piece standing on nothing (`floating-piece`, review 2's probe) and no gap in a roof, walkable
or not (`roof-hole`). While the fix lanes work they are reported, not failed: `Gate.DEFAULT` is `REPORT`, the test run
writes `build/reports/gate/`, and `hearth/gate/known/<category>.txt` lists the archetypes that break each rule today,
one file per lane's categories. Each lane deletes its entries as its fixes land. **At wave exit the coordinator sets
`Gate.DEFAULT = Mode.HARD`**: from then on a rule broken by an archetype not listed for it fails the tests. Review 2's
probe also runs over whole towns: `townPreview` writes `..._floating.txt` (each plot on the town's own land, then the
composed town, where a neighbour's cut can take the ground from under a piece).

Metric quirks of §2 fixed with the gate: lattice and shutter windows count; round bodies are measured per column;
decorative cauldrons (no POI beside them, in an open space or under the sky) are not workstations; a tie beam is not a floor; the dock is measured on
its shore and the walkway between two house fronts (`gametest/audit/Demos.java`). `fall_edge_cells` no longer counts a ladder's
rungs beside open air or a step from the floor's level onto the street before the plot (the synthetic slope and cliff
fall away there; the planner lays the street at the floor). `roof-hole` reads a gap only where built blocks (not the
hillside, not a railing) close it and open air lies under it: the gallows' drop, decks and hillside pockets were read
as gaps in the first tally (roof-gap cases 1012 -> 814 of 11256 on the wave-7 `hearth`). The audit columns `strict_reach`,
`floating_pieces`, `roof_gap_cells`, `gate` and `gate_text` are new and do not enter the scores; the scores' weights are
unchanged, but the quirk fixes move some rows from §3 (karst and tropical windows, the round house, wells and the
