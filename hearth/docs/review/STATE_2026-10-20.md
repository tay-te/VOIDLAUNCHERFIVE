## 3. Ranked

**Improved**

1. Playability: the measured blockers are gone outside the castle and cathedral; play mean 87 to 94, worst cases 73 to 86.
2. Walled hearts (temperate, desert) and the mediterranean piazza now read as town centres with a square and market.
3. Night lighting and furnished interiors: warm, full, no dark rooms.
4. Less reskinning: 52 archetypes share one plan across 5+ cultures (was 67).

**Still weak**

1. **Town form outside walls.** Farmsteads and hillside layouts scatter; no continuous street rows, party walls face grass. Unchanged since review 2.
2. **Landmark siting.** Bell, clock towers, cathedral and castle stand at the edges or alone; hill tops stay bare.
3. **No belt of fields.** A city of ~210 has a handful of fields; towns stop dead at grass or scrub.
4. **Drops and roof gaps.** 38 archetypes with unguarded drops, 41 with roof gaps (gate FAILs, mostly known).
5. **Castle and keep** remain the worst big buildings (play 60 and 67); palace worst case 45.
6. **Detail** barely moved (70.8 to 72.7): flat facades, detail under 50 for palisade, walkway, statue, garden, tilt yard, terrace field.
